HOW XP WORKS

Every completed mission is worth 10 XP. No multipliers, no bonuses.

Level 1 to 2 needs 100 XP. Each level after that needs 10 XP more than the last (level 2 to 3 needs 110; level 99 to 100 needs 1,080).

Reaching level 100 takes 58,410 XP in total. Each day you get 5 missions, up to 3 extra missions, and up to 3 XP-earning side jobs. Missing a day only resets your streak, never your XP.
